# Identity Standard

Identity defines how humans, agents, devices, applications, and services are represented across Web4hub systems.

## Principles

- Identity should be portable across compatible services.
- Authentication and authorization are separate concerns.
- Cryptographic proofs should be verifiable without exposing unnecessary private data.
- Recovery must not require copying secrets into application databases.

Implementations may use different credentials, but interoperability requires a stable identity representation and proof contract.
